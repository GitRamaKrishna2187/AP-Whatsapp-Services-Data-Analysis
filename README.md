# AP-Whatsapp-Services-Data-Analysis
AndhraPradesh Government has launched a new medium of giving services to Public via whatsapp. The Govt wants to add more number of services in whatsapp domain so that the people get benefitted without the need of physical presence to Govt offices.This Project deals with Analysing Most used services by the Public under the Existing APSeva portal
The Project has 4 Dashboards                                                                                                                               **Dashboard1** Total Service Requests Generated , Revenue generated for corresponding service requests. Revenue reconciled to the treasury by the concerned Operators, Reconcilation mode (Bill desk/UPI/Challan), Service with zero amounts for a period of 01-10-2019 to 15-09-2025 ( Approx period of 5.5 years)            **Dashboard2:** Services requests as well as Revenue generated from District to Secretariat wise. Using drill down option we can drill down to bottom most level **Dashboard3**: Top 10 Districts/Mandals/Secretariat wise Performance in terms of Services requests raised as well as revenue generated.                                  Note: Service requests raised is not proportional to revenue generated                                                                                            **Dashboard4:** Comparison of Service requests Generated,Approval and Rejection ratio in APSEVA & Whatsapp domains                                                    **Dashboard5:** Revenue Generated Department wise in Andhra Pradesh state government over the period of 5.5 years                                                     **Dashboard6:** Top 10 Services Revenue generation, their corresponding number of service requests and percentage of each service among the total revenue generation  **Dashboard7:** Whatsapp services requests raised during the span of 9 months from the day of launch
